# Product photo shot list

Print this and keep it by the backdrop. Photos sell used guitars: buyers can't hold the
guitar, so the photos have to answer their questions, and show every flaw before they
ask.

## Set-up (the same every time)

- [ ] One backdrop for every product: a plain wall or a large card in one of the site's
      colours (deep blue, rosewood brown or the pale blue-grey).
- [ ] One light set-up: daylight from a window at your side (not behind the guitar), or
      two cheap softbox lights at 45° either side. No direct sun, no flash.
- [ ] Wipe the phone lens. Turn HDR on. Don't use zoom: walk closer instead.
- [ ] Clean the guitar: fingerprints and dust show up close.
- [ ] Hide anything that shows where you live (windows, street views, letters, mirrors).

## Every guitar

- [ ] Full front, whole guitar in frame. This is the main photo: upload it first.
- [ ] Full back.
- [ ] Headstock, front.
- [ ] Headstock, back, with the **serial number readable**.
- [ ] Body close-up, at an angle so the finish catches the light.
- [ ] Pickups and controls.
- [ ] Neck joint (neck plate or heel).
- [ ] Frets and fretboard, showing fret wear.
- [ ] The case or gig bag, if included.
- [ ] **Every flaw**, close up: dings, scratches, buckle rash, worn finish, replaced parts.

## Gear (pedals, amps, parts)

- [ ] Front, top and back panels.
- [ ] Every input, output and power socket.
- [ ] Serial or model label.
- [ ] Any wear, and everything that's included (box, cables, manual).

## Shoot each one twice

- [ ] Landscape **and** portrait: the shop crops differently on phones and computers.

## Uploading

- Upload in the order above; the first photo is the main one, and you can reorder later.
- Add a short description to each photo in English and Arabic (for example "Headstock,
  back" / "رأس الجيتار من الخلف"); screen readers read it out.
- Upload from the admin only. The upload removes GPS location from the photo; never post
  the original files anywhere else.
